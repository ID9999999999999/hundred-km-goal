# Privacy Policy Draft — 100KM Goal

100KM Goal records walking and running sessions on the user's device.

## Data Collected

- GPS route points during a started session.
- Distance, duration and average speed computed from the session.
- Date and time of saved sessions.

## Data Storage

All data is stored locally on the user's device. The prototype does not transmit data to any server.

## Third Parties

The map screen loads OpenStreetMap tiles when displaying a route. The app does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or social tracking.

## User Control

The user can delete saved sessions from the history screen.
